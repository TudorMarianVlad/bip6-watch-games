# Wristlings: creature ideas

Ideas for new Wristlings, not built yet. Each one is tied to something the watch
knows about you or the world, so that collecting them keeps rewarding real life.

**Sensor check needed:** the Zepp OS 4 API has all the sensors below, but not every
watch has every sensor. Before building one, confirm the Bip 6 has it
(the barometer and compass especially).

**Built (2026-11-14):** Moonmew → Lunacat (changed to night only, not full moon), Slothnap, Drizzlet → Stormjelly,
Sunbun → Solarabbit, Kindlepig → Furnasow, Dimmouse, Mistlantern (fog, or nights after 8,000 steps),
Aurowl (face north), Needlefin → Lodestar (face where it points or it flees), Dawnlark (face east at dawn; new idea).
They are in the game; the rest below are still ideas.

## 1. Weather (`Weather` forecast)

| Name | Element | Look | How you meet it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Drizzlet** → **Stormjelly** | Water | A tiny raincloud with jellyfish tendrils of rain; evolves into a storm cloud with crackling tendrils | Only when it is **raining** where you are |
| **Sunbun** → **Solarabbit** | Grass | A bunny with sunflower-petal ears that turn to face the sun | Only on **clear, sunny** days |
| **Mistlantern** | Shadow | A fog sprite carrying a little lantern | Only in **fog or mist** |
| **Snowbell** → **Chimefrost** | Ice | A bell made of snow that rings when it moves | Only when it is **snowing** |
| **Kitewing** → **Galeray** | Air | A kite-shaped manta ray with a string tail | Only on **windy** days |

Battle twist: while it rains, Water moves could hit 10% harder, and Fire moves 10% softer (and the other way round in sunshine).

## New mechanics these ideas could bring

- **Status effects:** Burn (loses HP each turn), Freeze (skips a turn), Sleep (Hushling, Snowbell).
- **Weather in battle:** rain, sun and snow boost or weaken matching elements.
- **New ways to evolve:** weekly PAI (Kindlepig), distance walked as lead (Trekkit), stand hours (Totemite), instead of only levels.
- **Dual elements:** e.g. Stormjelly as Water + Electric, which makes the type chart more interesting.
- **A new LIGHT element** to pair with Shadow (Dimmouse could become its opposite, a "Glowmouse").

## My picks to build first

1. **Slothnap**: no sensor needed, so they work everywhere and are easy to test.
2. **Drizzlet** and **Sunbun**: weather is the most "magical" link to real life.
3. **Kindlepig**: the PAI evolution rewards a whole week of activity.
